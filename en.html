E-ink & digital detox

Open-source tools for long-form reading, listening, and digital wellbeing. High contrast, no ads, no accounts, no algorithm. Designed for e-ink screens and intentional use. Since 2026, the Reader's family: a whole phone in black and white, text only.

The Reader's family

A phone that reads like a page. Black-and-white, text-only apps: no icons, no colours, no cards, no account. The launcher shows words; the calendar, tasks, notes, books and feeds read like a newspaper. In the spirit of the Light Phone, on any Android. All of them ship from the F-Droid repository below, in English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian. Each also provides standard black-and-white widgets for any other launcher.

Reader's Launcher

v1.14.0 · MIT · com.freedomfighter.readerslauncher

The home screen as words: apps and categories one line each, a grid of three to five key apps at the bottom, and two-line built-in tiles — clock, weather (MeteoSwiss in Switzerland), agenda, tasks, notes, the book being read, the Littré's word of the day, a mindfulness bowl, a recorder. Swipe-up app drawer with search, app shortcuts on double tap. Two e-book reading slots left and right of home — or, on the right, further home pages, the book moving after the last one. Fixed pages, never scrolling ones: when a page is full, it is full.

Reader's Calendar

Android v1.8.0 · Linux v1.6.0 · MIT · com.freedomfighter.readerscalendar

A monochrome calendar on the phone's own calendars. Agenda as a list; month; week and workdays as time grids in both orientations — solid blocks, the white between them is your free time; the day as a grid; the event page in two columns. The calendar an event belongs to is a quiet word, never a colour. Creating an event is a tap away everywhere: a row at the bottom of every view, an empty slot, a long press on a day. The Linux version keeps the landscape layout on CalDAV (Infomaniak, Nextcloud, Radicale) and reads Google calendars through their secret iCal address.

Reader's Tasks

Android v1.3.0 · Linux v1.3.0 · MIT · com.freedomfighter.readerstasks

CalDAV task lists (Infomaniak, Tasks.org Cloud, Nextcloud, Radicale). The lists, the open tasks, a box to tick, a line to add. Drag to reorder, the order is saved on the server. Done tasks hidden, reopenable. The launcher reads its lists in its tile. The same app on Linux, one window.

Reader's Notes

v1.2.0 · MIT · com.freedomfighter.readersnotes

Plain-text notes, one list and one page. The first line of a note is its name. Each note is a .txt file in a WebDAV folder — kDrive, Nextcloud, any server — synced both ways, with a copy on conflict: a computer sees ordinary files. Text shared from another app becomes a note.

Reader's Books

v1.2.0 · MIT · com.freedomfighter.readersbooks

A shelf as a list and pages of text cut at whole lines, never through one. EPUB, MOBI, FB2, plain text, opened from the app, a file manager or a share. Right half to turn, left half to go back, long press for chapters and text size. Position remembered per book, screen kept on while reading.

Download APK Source

Reader's Recorder

v1.0.0 · MIT · com.freedomfighter.readersrecorder

An answer to the Pixel Recorder that never goes through Google. A memo, a lecture or a conversation in one tap (list, widget, launcher tile), pause and stop from the notification. By default everything stays on the phone. In three questions a WebDAV cloud folder (kDrive, Nextcloud…) receives each recording: your own computer cleans it, normalises its loudness and transcribes it with WhisperX (speakers told apart in a conversation), then the transcript and the cleaned audio come back to the phone. The phone's language, English, or detection.

Download APK Source

Reader's Mindful Tool

v1.0.0 · MIT · com.freedomfighter.readersmindful

Three things a meditator asks of a bowl: a single strike every X minutes until you stop it, a meditation of X minutes closed by three bowls, and one or three bowls at set times every day. Retreat Timer's five bowls or your own mp3/wav file. The same hardening as Retreat Timer: exact alarm-clock alarms, a foreground service, the alarm stream, re-arming after a reboot — the bowl sounds on time, even from deep sleep. Standard widget (play/stop, minutes, countdown, progress) and a launcher tile.

Download APK Source

Reader's Feeds

v1.1.0 · GPL-3.0 · com.freedomfighter.readersfeeds

An RSS/Atom reader born from Pluralis and redrawn in the family's look. The latest articles of your sources in one list, articles read as pages extracted from the web, sources, articles saved for later, OPML import and export, paid Substacks. Paged lists instead of scrolling for e-ink screens.

Installation

  1. Install the F-Droid client.
  2. Tap "Add to F-Droid" above — confirm the pre-filled address.
  3. Refresh the catalog, search for the app, install.

Option 2 — direct APK

  1. Allow installs from unknown sources for your browser.
  2. Tap "Download APK" under the app you want.
  3. Open the file and confirm. No auto-updates with this method.

Meditation

Three Android apps for a retreat: each does one thing, and nothing interrupts it — not deep sleep, not airplane mode, not another sound. In the F-Droid repository above.

Retreat Timer

v1.13.0 · MIT · com.freedomfighter.retreattimer

Rings a recording of singing-bowl bells at the times of day you choose — the signal to begin or end a sitting — so a retreat keeps its schedule with no one keeping watch. Each time rings three bells or a single one, from five bowls or your own mp3/wav file. Built for one job: the bells ring no matter what, even from deep sleep.

Download APK Source

Retreat Player

v1.5.0 · MIT · com.freedomfighter.retreatplayer

Plays MP3s of dhamma talks, bells and chanting during a retreat, on the same principle as Retreat Timer: once playback starts, nothing interrupts it. Every recording is copied into the app when loaded, so playback needs no network — airplane mode is fine. A foreground service holding a wake lock.

Download APK Source

Retreat Walk

v1.0.0 · MIT · com.freedomfighter.retreatwalk

Rings a singing bowl at a fixed interval — every ten minutes, say — over and over until you stop it. For switching partners in a paired exercise, or for sitting with a regular reminder to stay awake. One number and one button: that is the whole app.

Download APK Source

PageTurn

v1.0.0 · Manifest v3 + bookmarklet

Turns scrollable web pages into paginated reading — like a book. Instant page turns (no animation, essential on e-ink). Auto-detects articles on Substack, Medium, WordPress, Ghost, Dev.to, and more. Strips ads, sidebars, pop-ups, and comments. Keyboard, swipe, and edge-tap navigation.

Download (ZIP) Source code

Social Media Blocker

v1.0 · Chrome / Firefox (Manifest v3)

Blocks social media and video-sharing sites. To unblock, solve five math problems of increasing difficulty — designed to break the automatic reflex without making sites entirely inaccessible. Zero data collection.

Download (ZIP, Chrome and Firefox) Source code

Installation

  1. Download and extract the ZIP.
  2. Chrome: chrome://extensions/ → Developer mode → Load unpacked → select folder.
  3. Firefox: about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox → Load Temporary Add-on → pick manifest.firefox.json (Social Media Blocker) or manifest.json.

ThinkBook Plus Gen 4 IRU · Python

Linux GUI for the color e-ink display of the Lenovo ThinkBook Plus Gen 4 IRU. Supports GNOME, KDE Plasma, Cinnamon, XFCE (X11 and Wayland). Handles display routing between OLED and e-ink panels, frontlight and brightness via the embedded controller.

GPL-3.0 · Python / PyQt5

E-book reader for the ThinkBook e-ink display. Page mode only (no scroll), sends clear-ghosts commands to Tinta4PlusU after each page turn. EPUB, MOBI, PDF, DJVU, FB2, CBR/CBZ, Markdown, plain text.
