ePub Magazine reader

v0.2.5 · GPL-3.0 · ua.acclorite.book_story.debug

ePub viewer tuned for daily newspaper and magazine issues. Auto-detects magazine ePubs on open, paginates the table of contents with one card per article (section, title, optional cover image), and renders each article in a vertically-scrolled view. Tap-driven navigation (left / right turn the page, centre toggles the toolbar). Calibrated against the ePub formats produced for NZZ, 24 heures, Courrier International, and The Economist.

Installation

Option 1 — via F-Droid (recommended, auto-updates)

  1. Install the F-Droid client if you don't already have it.
  2. From the Android device, tap “Add to F-Droid” above. F-Droid opens its "add repository" page with the address and fingerprint pre-filled — confirm.
  3. Refresh the catalog (refresh icon), then search for the app you want and install it like any other.

Manual fallback if the deep link doesn't work: in F-Droid > Settings > Repositories > +, paste the address and fingerprint shown above.

Option 2 — direct APK (one-shot install)

  1. Allow installs from unknown sources for your browser (Settings > Apps > your browser > Install unknown apps).
  2. Tap "Download APK" under the app you want.
  3. Open the downloaded file and confirm the install.

Updates are not automatic with this method — check back here periodically.
